## Timezone feasibility gate

A fixed Intl probing grid cannot prove arbitrary unseen transitions or disjoint local-date membership. The plan's approved fallback therefore uses exact-pinned `@js-temporal/polyfill` 0.5.1, with JSBI 4.3.2 resolved in the lockfile. This follows the library's [maintainer documentation](https://github.com/js-temporal/temporal-polyfill), [release record](https://github.com/js-temporal/temporal-polyfill/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md), and Temporal's [PlainDate conversion](https://tc39.es/proposal-temporal/docs/plaindate.html#toZonedDateTime) and [start-of-day semantics](https://tc39.es/proposal-temporal/docs/zoneddatetime.html#startOfDay).

The six literal UTC intervals and immediate endpoint membership checks passed before metric query implementation. They cover UTC, New York's 23/25-hour days, repeated Havana midnight, skipped Sao Paulo midnight, and Apia's skipped date. They are regression fixtures, not a claim that six samples prove every timezone history. Timezone availability follows this host's ICU data and the pinned library's semantics. No single-offset or 24-hour approximation is used. Library licenses remain in their distributed packages and lock metadata, ISC for the polyfill and Apache-2.0 for JSBI.
